# Beans voice

Landing copy and video captions follow this guide. App copy already does.

## How Beans sounds

Plain, specific and warm, with a light garden touch. Beans talks like a friend who keeps a good notebook, not a company with an announcement.

| Rule | Write | Not |
|---|---|---|
| Say what happens | "Unfinished tasks move to today at midnight." | "Never lose track of your priorities." |
| Name the real thing | "Drag a task onto the Timebox to give it a time." | "Intelligent scheduling for your workflow." |
| Use short sentences | "Spill it all out. Sort it later." | "Capture everything on your mind so you can organize it whenever you're ready." |
| Talk to one person | "your day" | "teams and individuals" |
| Keep the garden light | One garden touch per section, at most | Puns in every line |
| Use statements | "Your whole week on one board." | "What if your week fit on one board?" |

## Words

**Product names, always capitalized:** Beans, Brain Dump, Timebox, Quick Capture, Focus, Pomodoro, Trash.

**Garden words that are welcome, sparingly:** spill, plant, row, sprout, grow, roll over, tend, patch.

**Never use:** AI-powered, supercharge, unlock, seamless, effortless, game-changer, revolutionary, productivity hack, world-class, next-level, 10x, journey, empower, elevate, streamline, reimagine, all-in-one.

**Avoid:** "simply", "just", "easily", and exclamation marks. One exclamation mark on a whole page is the limit.

## Facts you can state

Each of these works today in the app.

- Brain Dump and Lists hold tasks without a date.
- The board has one column per day and keeps scrolling.
- The Timebox shows the day's hours as a beanstalk, and tasks drag onto it. Blocks resize.
- A leaf marks the current time.
- Quick Capture reads labels, dates and times as you type, such as `#home`, `tomorrow` and `=30m`.
- Tasks have notes, subtasks, labels, priority, due dates, estimates and actual time.
- Timer and Pomodoro. Actual time can fill in from the timer.
- Daily planning and daily wrap-up rituals, each optional.
- Analytics by label, with a table view.
- Unfinished tasks roll to today at your local midnight, and show a "Rolled over" tag.
- Repeating tasks and templates.
- Trash with undo. Items stay 30 days.
- Search, filter, keyboard shortcuts, and light and dark themes.
- Changes sync live between open windows.
- A phone layout in the browser.

## Facts you can't state

These aren't true or aren't decided. Leave them out.

- Calendar account sync (Google, Outlook and the like), attachments, notifications, import or export.
- A desktop app, native mobile apps, or offline use.
- AI features or integrations of any kind.
- Any price, "free", a free trial, or a number of users.
- Any testimonial, rating, press mention or customer logo.

## Calls to action

- Video outro: "Spill the beans." with **Start planning** beneath it.

## Sample lines

Use these as a pattern. Write fresh copy for each section.

- "Spill the beans." (the empty Brain Dump prompt, and the brand line)
- "Your tasks, your week and your hours in one place."
- "Plan the day. Give each task a time. Watch it grow."
- "Whatever you don't finish moves to today. No guilt, no copying things over."
