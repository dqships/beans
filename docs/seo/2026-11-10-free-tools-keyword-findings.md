## Reading of the numbers

This section is interpretation, not tool data.

- **Best odds for a new site:** brain dump, time blocking planner, break timer, Eisenhower matrix and habit tracker. Their first pages are paper products, PDFs, Pinterest, Etsy or one-page sites, and none of the top results is a working tool that saves what you type.
- **Biggest volume, hardest to win:** the time, date and time card calculators. Calculator sites that have ranked for years hold those pages. They are worth having because the terms are large and the tools are quick to build well, but expect them to take months.
- **Closest to what Beans sells:** time blocking planner, daily planner template, weekly schedule maker, brain dump, to-do list and Pomodoro timer. Each one is a small piece of the planner, so the step from the tool to a trial is short.
- **Furthest from Beans:** sleep calculator and date calculator. They are in because the volume is large and they are honest planning questions (when to stop for the day, how long until a deadline), but they will convert worst.
- Every result page had an AI Overview. For terms with a one-line answer ("how many hours in a week" is 168), expect most searchers never to click. That page earns its place through the weekly time budget under the answer.
